SENTRA - SIF precursor detection for Oil India Limited (Problem Statement 26165)

WHAT IS INSTALLED
  The SENTRA console: sign-in, the HSE workspace (Home, Ingest, Dashboard,
  HSE Review with the decision trail, Action Items, Risk Hotspots map, Asset
  Memory, Profile) and Administration (Engines, Settings, SysLog, Audit Log,
  New HSE Login, Data & Backup).

WHERE YOUR DATA LIVES
  Accounts, the hash-chained audit trail, review decisions, the compliance
  calendar, the SQL database (sentra.db), sealed secrets and settings are kept
  in %APPDATA%\SIF Insight Console - not in the program folder. Uninstalling
  SENTRA does not delete them.

  The first time SENTRA starts on this computer it asks you to create the
  administrator account. There is no default password.

FONTS
  Inter, JetBrains Mono and IBM Plex are included under the SIL Open Font
  Licence 1.1; the licence texts are in ui\assets\fonts in the program folder.
